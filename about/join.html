Join First Signs

Membership is free. Your name and a valid email are required; nothing about your child's health is asked at registration.

Family member

Free · For parents and caregivers. No health data required to register.

  • Access to the family community when it opens
  • Printable checklists and guides
  • Member benefits from partner doctors and hospitals
  • Invitations to expert Q&A sessions

Doctor member

Free · For medical professionals. Verified Doctor badges are granted only by a First Signs admin after credential checks.

  • Verified Doctor badge on forum replies
  • Listing on the doctor panel after verification and formal agreement
  • Participation in expert videos and Q&A sessions

Register

Doctor accounts receive the Verified Doctor badge only after a First Signs admin confirms credentials. Email sign-in links and Google sign-in connect at publication; in this draft, registration itself is real and saved to the site's member store. Consent wording is a placeholder for counsel review.

What happens with your details

  • Your name and email are stored in the First Signs member store, visible to the site admin only.
  • Your name appears with your community messages and posts. Your email is never shown publicly.
  • No health information is required, at registration or ever.
  • You can ask for your details to be corrected or deleted at any time through the contact page.
