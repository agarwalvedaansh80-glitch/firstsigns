Dr. Jeffrey Fearon

MD; Director, The Craniofacial Center, Dallas

Craniofacial surgery (practice devoted entirely to children with craniofacial conditions) · Dallas, Texas, USA

Role with First Signs: International medical reviewer for First Signs craniosynostosis content; performed Vedaansh's corrective surgery at 14 months

Qualifications

  • BA, Brown University; post-baccalaureate science studies, Columbia University
  • MD, University of Cincinnati College of Medicine
  • General surgery residency, Deaconess/Harvard Fifth Surgical Service
  • Plastic surgery residency, Massachusetts General Hospital (Harvard Medical School affiliation)
  • Craniofacial surgery fellowship, Children's Hospital of Philadelphia (University of Pennsylvania)
  • Board certification: [to be confirmed]

Current positions

  • Director, The Craniofacial Center, Dallas, Texas
  • President emeritus, American Society of Craniofacial Surgeons
  • President emeritus, Texas Society of Plastic Surgeons
  • Former Associate Editor, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery

Experience

More than 30 years in a focused craniofacial practice; patients from over 40 countries; in-office and videoconference consultations

Notable work

  • One of the few craniofacial surgeons in the world whose entire practice is dedicated to craniofacial surgery
  • Holder of a US patent for a craniofacial bone distraction device
  • Member of the Robert M. Goldwyn Guild

Selected publications

  • Over 100 peer-reviewed articles; H-index of 38 per Google Scholar (as stated by the centre)
  • Fearon JA, McLaughlin EB, Kolar JC. Sagittal craniosynostosis: surgical outcomes and long-term growth. Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery 117:532, 2006
  • Fearon JA. Evidence-Based Medicine: Craniosynostosis. Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery 133(5):1261-1275, 2014
  • Fearon JA, Ditthakasem K, Herbert M, et al. Thirty-Year Experience Treating Syndromic Craniosynostosis. Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery 155(1):131-137, 2024
