Questions to ask your doctor

You do not need to become a medical expert to be an effective partner in your child's care. Take this list to your appointment.

Your journey · 7 of 7
←→
Questions to ask your doctor

About the diagnosis

  • What do you think is causing the head shape?
  • Which suture is involved, if any?
  • Is this confirmed or still being investigated?
  • Are there other possible explanations?

About tests

  • What test is being recommended?
  • What question will the test answer?
  • Who will interpret the result?
  • Should another specialist review it?

About the team

  • Which specialists should see my child?
  • Who coordinates the care?
  • Who should we contact if we have a question between appointments?

About treatment

  • Is treatment necessary?
  • Why is it recommended at this age?
  • What options are appropriate?
  • What are the risks, benefits and limitations?
  • What will recovery involve?
  • What follow-up will be needed?

About costs (India)

  • What will the full treatment cost, in a written estimate?
  • Which scheme or insurance routes does this hospital accept, and who helps us file?
  • What costs continue after discharge: medicines, helmet if prescribed, follow-up visits, travel?

About the future

  • How will we monitor development?
  • Are there other assessments that may be needed?
  • What should we watch for as our child grows?

And one final question is always reasonable: "Can you please explain that again in simpler language?"

Understanding your child's care is not disrespectful. It is part of being an informed parent.
