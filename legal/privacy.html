Legal

Privacy policy

First Signs is built to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act). The principles below are built into the product; the full legal text will be finalised by counsel before launch.

Our privacy principles

  • Clear consent at sign-up. Registration asks for explicit consent, in plain language, before any personal data is stored.
  • Minimal data. An account needs a name, an email and a role. Nothing more is required.
  • No health data required to register. You can use every information page, and register, without telling us anything about your child's health.
  • Anonymous participation. The forum supports anonymous display names, and posting identifiable child photos, scans or medical records in public posts is blocked by the rules.
  • Children's privacy. Stories involving children are published only with the written consent of a parent or guardian, and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Your rights. You can ask for a copy of your data, correction, or deletion of your account, through the official contact address.

What this draft stores

In this draft preview, registrations (name, email, role, city if given) and community messages and posts are saved to the site's private member store so the community works end to end. The registration list is visible only to the site admin. At publication this store is replaced by the production database under the final privacy notice, and this draft's data is not carried over without consent.
