Medical disclaimer

First Signs provides general educational and family-navigation information.

It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, interpret an individual child's scan, or replace a consultation with a qualified medical professional. Information may change as medical guidance develops. Families should rely on their treating healthcare team for decisions concerning their child's individual care.

If you believe your child has an urgent medical problem, seek appropriate urgent medical care rather than waiting for a response through this website.

What this means in practice

  • Content on this site is educational, written for families, and reviewed under our medical governance process. It is general information about a condition, never advice about a specific child.
  • Doctor replies in the community forum are labelled "General information, not a medical consultation" because that is exactly what they are.
  • The doctor panel is a navigation directory. Listing is not endorsement, and no ranking is expressed or implied.
  • Family stories are lived experience, labelled as such, and are not medical guidance.
  • Figures on costs, schemes and insurance are indicative and dated; the numbers that matter are the written answers from your hospital, insurer or scheme desk.
