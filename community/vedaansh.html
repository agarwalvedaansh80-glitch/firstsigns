Vedaansh's journey

"I was once the child whose parents were looking for answers."

Vedaansh Agarwal was born with left unilateral coronal craniosynostosis. His parents first noticed that one eyelid did not close fully during sleep. The family consulted doctors and underwent investigations. The early diagnostic journey involved several specialties before the condition was correctly identified.

At 14 months, Vedaansh underwent corrective surgery in Dallas, performed by Dr. Jeffrey Fearon at Medical City Children's Hospital. His surgery was successful, and genetic testing later described his case as sporadic rather than inherited.

Today Vedaansh is a Grade 11 student in Mumbai, and the founder and Youth Ambassador of First Signs. He started it so that the next Indian family who notices something about their baby's head does not have to search the way his family did.

Vedaansh's story is the reason First Signs begins with craniosynostosis.

But his story is not a template for another child. Every child is different. Every diagnosis is different. Every family receives advice based on its own medical circumstances.

Family stories on First Signs are labelled as lived experience. They can tell you what another family felt, what questions they asked and what helped them emotionally. They cannot tell you what your child's treatment should be.
