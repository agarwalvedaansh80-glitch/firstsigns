Share your family's story

If you would like to share your story, First Signs uses a consent-based process and clearly distinguishes personal experience from medically reviewed information.

How stories are handled: submissions are reviewed before publication; identifying details are included only with written consent; stories about children use the consent of parents or guardians; medical claims inside stories are either removed or clearly marked as the family's experience rather than medical fact.
